# Vision

This project explores how automotive companies can systematically prepare for AI adoption by:
- Collecting and structuring data
- Preparing data for AI use
- Objectively scoring and prioritizing AI use cases
- Executing AI pipelines in a modular, scalable way

The focus is on the realities of German automotive IT landscapes and how an Omniverse-inspired approach could bridge the gap.
This vision has been written by an engineer with above 8 years of real life experiences within German "Premium Automotive" experience.
